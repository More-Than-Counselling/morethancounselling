About our services

More Than Counselling provides holistic, integrative counselling, nervous system education, and therapeutic movement such as yoga. This work supports your emotional and physical wellbeing and may sit alongside, but does not replace, medical, psychiatric, or other treatment you are receiving.

Counselling is not a crisis or emergency service. Fionna is not available for between-session crisis support and cannot guarantee an immediate response to messages.

If you need urgent help

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, please call 000. For crisis support at any time, contact Lifeline on 13 11 14, the Suicide Call Back Service on 1300 659 467, or attend your nearest emergency department.

Scope of practice

Fionna works within the scope of her training and professional ethical guidelines. Counselling does not include the diagnosis of medical or psychiatric conditions, the prescribing of medication, or the provision of legal or medical advice.

Where your needs fall outside this scope, or would be better met elsewhere, Fionna will discuss this with you and, where appropriate, support you to find a suitable referral.

Telehealth

Sessions are available in person or via telehealth. If you choose telehealth, you are responsible for having a private, safe space and a reliable internet connection. If a connection is lost, Fionna will attempt to reconnect, and the session will continue for the remaining booked time.

Telehealth may not be appropriate in all circumstances. Fionna will discuss with you whether it is a suitable option for your needs.
